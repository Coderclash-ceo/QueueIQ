The Conflict in Outpatient Care

Appointments vs. Walk-In Demand

Hospitals handle two opposite streams: pre-booked patients who reserved a time slot, and unscheduled walk-in patients who just arrived. Combining them fairly has always been broken.

🛑 The Starvation Trap

If senior citizens and emergencies keep walking in, a patient who booked an appointment days ahead gets pushed back forever (Starvation).

⏳ Zero Visibility

Patients have no idea where they stand, wait blindly in crowded waiting halls, and overwhelm staff with anxiety questions.

Our Solution

QueueIQ: Unified Priority Engine

Inspired by OS CPU scheduling algorithms, QueueIQ reconciles appointments, walk-ins, seniors, and VIPs into a single deterministic priority queue.

🎯

Unified Single Queue

No special bypasses or hidden desk lines. A single mathematical sorting function sequences every patient.

⚙️

Anti-Starvation Aging

As a patient waits, their priority tier dynamically rises every 5 minutes. No patient can ever be starved.

The Core Algorithm (priority.js)

4-Tier Priority + Aging Formula

effectiveTier = min(3, baseTier + floor(minutesWaited / 5))

Tier 3: VIP / Doctor-Referred (Staff authorized only)
Immediate Head
Tier 2: Senior Citizen (Self-declared)
High Priority
Tier 1: Pre-booked Appointment (Checked in on arrival)
Scheduled Slot
Tier 0: Normal Walk-in (Starts at 0 → Ages to Tier 3)
+1 Tier / 5 Mins
